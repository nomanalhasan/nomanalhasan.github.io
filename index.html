About

Rigor, discipline, and regulatory depth

I'm a CFA Charterholder and Fellow Chartered Certified Accountant (FCCA) with additional qualifications as an ACA (ICAEW) and FMVA (Corporate Finance Institute) — one of a small number of professionals worldwide to hold charters from both the CFA Institute and ACCA. Over 15+ years I've worked across corporate finance, financial planning, accounting, banking, taxation, and investment management.

My track record spans financial modeling and valuation, feasibility studies, budgeting, credit analysis, and risk assessment for clients in manufacturing, renewable energy, RMG/export garments, hospitality, and fintech. I currently serve as Managing Director of YAxis Advisory Ltd, CFO & Head of Fintech Product Strategy at Proficient Information Systems, and Strategic CFO of Sylvan Wye Resort & Agro PLC — alongside a long-standing role training CFA candidates and finance professionals at FPP Training & Consultancy.

A core focus of my practice is Bangladesh's NGO and microfinance regulatory environment — MRA compliance, NBR taxation, and ICAB audit standards — supporting institutional clients through audit readiness, MIS reporting, and financial governance.
